Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Gülümseyelim handles information when you connect a TikTok account. We collect only what is needed to provide and protect the service.

Information from TikTok

With your permission, we may receive your TikTok user ID, display name, avatar, and publicly available videos through the TikTok Login Kit and Display API. We do not request your TikTok password, direct messages, contacts, or private videos.

How we use it

We use this information to identify your connected account, show your authorized profile and public videos, provide support, and prevent abuse. We do not sell TikTok data or use it for targeted advertising.

Storage and retention

Access tokens are stored securely on our server and are never placed in browser code. We retain connected-account data only while your connection is active and delete it when you disconnect or when it is no longer needed, subject to legal requirements.

Your choices and deletion

You can revoke access in TikTok settings at any time. To ask us to disconnect your account or delete data associated with it, email privacy@gulumseyelim.com from the account email. We will confirm and complete eligible requests within 30 days.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact privacy@gulumseyelim.com.
